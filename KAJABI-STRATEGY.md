# Rock Rich on Kajabi — The Heart-of-the-Business Strategy

*Synthesized from 7 parallel research streams (Activation, Course Completion, Gamification, Pricing/Tiers, Engagement Cadence, Competitive Teardown, Content→Community Flywheel + Instrumentation). 2024–2026 benchmarks and named case studies. Built for Jason Rutland / 7 Kin Homestead.*

**The one-line thesis:** Jason does not have a content problem, an audience problem, or a pricing-floor problem. He has an **activation and instrumentation problem**. 1,332 of 1,366 members are stuck at progress_score 25 because they joined and never had a *first result* inside the community — and Jason can't see who's close to converting because Kajabi gives him no per-member engagement visibility. Every lever below is in service of two outcomes: **manufacture a first result, then see it.**

## 3. The Plan, by Lever

### 3.1 Activation — Breaking the Lurker Wall

**What the research says.** The 90-9-1 rule is empirically confirmed; Jason's 97% is severe-but-normal. The killers are structural, not content-driven: members who don't engage in the first **90 days are 60–70% more likely to churn**; first-year renewal is **75% vs 84%** overall. Structured onboarding lifts first-year retention **25–40%** and time-to-first-engagement **50%** ([i4a, 2025](https://www.i4a.com/blog/new-member-onboarding/)). Welcome emails hit **54.3% open rate** (4x promo). The [DigitalMarketer "Love Our Lurkers" campaign](https://www.digitalmarketer.com/blog/activate-community-members/) **activated 44% of silent members in 5 days** (first-time publishers +73.7%). Circle Platinum communities onboard **2x faster** and **76% require member introductions vs 57%** ([Circle 2024](https://circle.so/2024-creator-community-report)).

**Concrete actions for Rock Rich:**
- **The 7-Day Ignition Sequence (every new Greenhouse join):** Day 0 welcome within 5 min, single CTA. Day 1 the *required* one-sentence intro ("What's the #1 thing holding back your financial situation right now?" — Jason's voice, audience's exact entry anxiety). Day 3 quick-win poll. Day 5 live-event spotlight. Day 7 "What did you come here to solve?" with reply. Targets: 80% Day-1 email open, 30% intro-post completion, 15% start the starter mini-course.
- **The "Love Our Lurkers" win-back (the existing 1,332):** Email-announced first (the channel is email, not the feed — they don't log in). 5-day themed run with #RockRichReturns and a free-month-of-Garden prize for first-time posters. Use Founding 50 members as the Day-3/4 testimonial anchors (Joleen Sims).
- **Segment the 1,332 before blasting:** (A) never logged in → "we saved your spot," (B) logged in/never posted → one-question reply ask, (C) posted once/went silent → personal recognition + soft challenge invite. Do **not** send all 1,332 the same message.
- **Drive everyone toward progress_score 50.** Whatever action crosses 25→50 (first post or first lesson) becomes the singular CTA of every first-week touchpoint. Run the weekly MCP delta check; 25→50 movers are the hottest Garden-invite targets and currently invisible.

### 3.2 Course Completion — Turning 194 Transcripts into Finished Outcomes

**What the research says.** Median self-paced completion is **12.6%** and hasn't improved in years. The lever is *not* content quality — it's social accountability: courses with active discussion hit **65.5% vs 42.6%** (+54%); cohort models reach **85–96%** ([altMBA 96%](https://medium.com/behavior-design/how-to-design-an-online-course-with-a-96-completion-rate-180678117a85)). **Week-one discussion activity = 14x more likely to complete** ([Ruzuku](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/articles/completion-gap)). Videos **5–9 min** retain best (MIT/Kajabi). Paid challenges complete at **70–80%**.

**Concrete actions:**
- **Micro-module architecture first.** Cap lessons at 5–9 min. Only ~30–40% of the 194 transcripts qualify as lessons — filter: *"contains one actionable system the viewer could implement this week?"* Lifestyle = marketing asset; system/howto/financial = lesson candidate.
- **Validate as a challenge before building a full course.** Package 5–7 transcripts on one self-contained system (e.g., the money-first homestead decision framework) → run as a $27–47 challenge → if 70%+ complete, rebuild as a Garden drip course. Don't build 10 courses nobody finishes.
- **Week-one gate:** Module 1 ends with a *required* post before Module 2 unlocks (Kajabi lesson automation can gate on a tag).
- **Lesson-completion automation stack:** on complete → tag + "you did it" email within minutes → after final lesson → 3-email graduation sequence pitching the next tier.
- **Cohort sprints for Founding 50:** 33 people is small enough for real-name accountability — the altMBA 96% benchmark is reachable. Quarterly 4-week sprint, live kickoff, hard end date.

### 3.3 Gamification — What Works vs Theater

**What the research says.** The PBL triad (Points-Badges-Leaderboards) bolted onto existing content is **theater** — meta-analysis d=0.52 overall, but effect *collapses* for PBL-alone, and stacking badges+leaderboards+coins **reduced** motivation in multiple studies. **Leaderboards are the most dangerous element** — they motivate only top performers and demotivate everyone below ([arXiv 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08551v1)). What works: **progress bars (#1, 60.5% preference)**, **milestone achievements (#2, 40.2%)**, the **Endowed Progress Effect** (pre-seed 2/10 → 34% faster completion), and informational feedback. Watch the **overjustification effect** — "points for posting" trains reward-dependence and collapses when rewards stop.

**Concrete actions — and what NOT to build:**
- **Do build:** (1) Progress bars on every course/onboarding journey. (2) **Endowed progress** — start every member at 2/10, never 0 (mark the intro module complete on enrollment). (3) **Milestone identity badges tied to real outcomes** — "First Sale," "Debt-Free Win," "Land Scout," "Systems Builder" — *not* "logged in." (4) The **Starting Line Quest**: 5 atomic, socially-safe steps (photo, intro sentence, react to someone, finish lesson 1, post a takeaway), always visible. (5) Feedback loops over reward loops — each module ends with a 2-question reflection that Jason or an anchor answers within 24h.
- **Do NOT build:** a full leaderboard ranking all 1,366 members (publicly displays 1,332 people at the bottom → mass disengagement); trivial-action badges; permanent ambient streaks for free members (you can't tie them to meaningful behavior pre-beacon). If you want competition, use **relative** rankings (5 above/5 below) or a non-ranked **"Wall of Wins."**
- **Narrative wrapper (Rock Rich lore):** Greenhouse = seeds, Garden = roots, Founding 50 = first-frost survivors. Quest = Chapter 1, first module = "The Soil Test." Narrative significantly outperforms PBL-alone.

### 3.4 Pricing & Tiers — Fixing the Ladder

**What the research says.** Engaged free members convert at **15–25%** (vs 2–5% untargeted). **44% of cancellations are in the first 90 days.** Annual subs churn **20–30% less, +25% LTV**. One-time top tiers are a cash-flow tool, not recurring revenue — **Founding 50 produces $0 MRR and no ascension path above $19.** Three tiers beat two/four (decoy effect). Reverse trials lift freemium→premium **10–40%**.

**Concrete actions tied to his real tiers:**

| Tier | Today | The fix |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Greenhouse (free, ~1,357)** | Over-serving lurkers, no magic moment | Engineer ONE trigger event (complete Module 1 of the free "Rock Rich Starting System"). On fire → 3-email ascension sequence within 7 days. Optionally lock Module 5 behind Garden — upgrade moment at peak engagement. |
| **Garden ($19/mo, ~36)** | Leaking LTV via 12 monthly churn decisions; no annual; survives the 90-day cliff by luck | **Add annual now: $190/yr** (2 months free), default the toggle to annual. Include all quarterly challenges + a monthly live Q&A as the retention hook. Converting 15 of 36 to annual = ~$2,850 ARR locked. |
| **Founding 50 ($297 one-time, 33)** | Dead-end ceiling, $0 MRR, no ongoing benefit | **Stop selling it.** Transition the 33 into a new recurring premium tier *at no cost, for life,* as the founding inner circle (grandfathered forever — that's the trust contract). They become the social-proof engine, not a dead tier. |
| **NEW: "Ranch Hand" / "Inner Circle" ($47–67/mo recurring)** | Missing rung | Direct-access only — weekly live Q&A, private channel, early course access, monthly ops briefing. Anchors Garden's $19 visually; gives Garden somewhere to climb. Launch by personally inviting the top 10 most-engaged Garden members (MCP can pull). |

**Reverse trial play:** grant the 1,332 lurkers (who've logged in ≥once in 90 days) 14 days of Garden access, then restrict. Loss aversion beats "here's what you're missing." Expect 10–40% conversion on those who actually log in.

### 3.5 Engagement Cadence — The Weekly Operating Rhythm

**What the research says.** High-engagement paid communities run on **predictable rhythm**, not sporadic drops. Only **15% of members are active in any 120-day window; 59% of posts get zero replies.** Small+paid+specialized communities hit **1.71% engagement vs 0.84% free**. One monthly live event "dramatically reduces churn." Digest emails hit **43–59% open** — the re-entry ramp for lurkers who never log in. A monthly leaderboard contest **tripled daily posts** in one Skool case.

**Concrete actions:**
- **The non-negotiable 3-post week:** **Monday** "This Week's Mission" prompt → **Wednesday** raw 60-sec voice/video check-in (unpolished = present) → **Friday** "Field Report" celebrating 3–5 member wins *by name*. Consistency beats volume.
- **One monthly live "Homestead Roundtable"** (45 min, unscripted — plays directly to his fence-neighbor voice). Replay posted *inside* the community to drive logins, not on YouTube.
- **Weekly digest automation** to Greenhouse members inactive 7+ days: "You missed this inside Rock Rich this week" — 3 best posts + 1 teaser.
- **Accountability pods (Garden + Founding 50):** groups of 8–12 by situation (debt payoff / land / side hustle), Monday goal → Friday result. Founding 50 members lead pods (Joleen Sims is the natural anchor). This is the "stay for the friendships" retention mechanism.

### 3.6 Content → Community Flywheel

**What the research says.** The flywheel runs: social → owned **email list** (never cold-jump straight to Kajabi) → free member → community wins → next content. Email from social converts at **19.3% landing-page rate** (highest of any source); creator email opens average **44%**. Comment-to-DM funnels convert **22–35% vs 2–4%** for passive bio links. Community questions are the best content-script source. Bundling = **4.5x revenue**.

**Concrete actions:**
- **Systematize the 725k TikTok pipeline Jason isn't using.** Every video's bio-link → a single-CTA landing page (one headline, one proof, one button) → **email opt-in** (lead magnet: "Rock Rich Budget Blueprint" / "7-Day Off-Grid Starter") → nurture sequence → Greenhouse invite → Day-0 activation. Even 1% of 725k clicking monthly = ~7,250 visits → ~362 new Greenhouse members/month at a 5% join rate.
- **Comment-to-DM:** "Comment SYSTEM and I'll send you the free guide" → auto-DM the lead magnet → capture email before they touch Kajabi.
- **Win Wednesday:** weekly 60-sec Short featuring a community win (with permission). Rewards the member, signals lurkers that results are real, creates social proof at zero extra production cost.
- **Question harvest:** pull the top 3 unanswered Greenhouse questions weekly → straight into the content calendar. Same pain points across 725k followers and 1,332 members.
- **Founder visibility:** one external post/month mentioning Rock Rich with an inside story ("a member asked me this week…") — cheapest acquisition channel he has.

### 3.7 Instrumentation — Making It Data-Driven

**What the research says.** Kajabi natively tracks revenue/MRR/churn/aggregate course %. It does **NOT** provide: per-lesson completion (API or UI), any community engagement metric, or progress past **1,000 members** (Product Progress Report hard cap — Jason's top 366 are blind). There's **no native "first post" trigger.** This is the gap the custom beacon + a founder dashboard must fill.

**Concrete actions:** see Section 4 — this lever *is* the build.

## 4. How This Ties to the Build

The three already-scoped pillars map 1:1 onto the strategy. None of the high-leverage moves work without them.

**Pillar 1 — Course-gen from the 194 transcripts (the content factory).**
- Drives Activation (free Starting-Line mini-course), Completion (micro-modules), Pricing (the bundle = 4.5x), and the upgrade trigger (locked final module).
- **Build rule:** filter for actionability (~30–40% qualify), chunk to 5–9 min lessons, validate as a challenge before a full course.

**Pillar 2 — The completion beacon (closing the reporting blind spot).**
- This is the **prerequisite for the most powerful mechanics.** Without it Jason cannot trigger upgrade-at-completion (the single highest-intent moment) and members can mark lessons "done" without watching.
- **Wiring:** beacon fires on lesson complete (confirm video watched to X% first) → POST to the kre8r server → tag the contact in Kajabi via MCP (`completed:course-1-lesson-3` / `course-completer-hot`) → trigger the next nurture step or a personal DM ("You hit the 30% mark. Most people quit before here. You didn't.").
- **Gating rule:** until the beacon ships, gate ALL gamification to community-behavior signals (post/comment/react/attend) — never course-completion data.

**Pillar 3 — Gamification.**
- Build order is load-bearing: **(1)** micro-course architecture + progress bars → **(2)** Starting Line Quest (lurker activation) → **(3)** quarterly challenge (free→paid) → **(4)** beacon → **(5)** milestone badges tied to beacon data → **(6)** full narrative lore. **Do not skip to #6.**
- Turn on Kajabi native points now, but **do not enable a full leaderboard.**

**Existing kre8r dashboards — what must be instrumented (NorthΩr / MissionΩr):**

The founder dashboard must surface these 7 metrics (buildable from Kajabi CSV exports + the MCP sync that already runs):

| Metric | Target | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Weekly Active Members (% of Greenhouse) | >15% | MCP sync / login data |
| Day-7 Activation Rate (one action within 7 days) | >30% | Beacon + community data |
| Free→Paid Conversion (new Garden / new Greenhouse, monthly) | 3–5% → 8–10% post-onboarding | Kajabi + MCP |
| Garden MRR + MoM delta | growth | Kajabi |
| Garden churn rate | <5%/mo | Kajabi |
| Course completion by product | rising | **Beacon (Kajabi can't show this)** |
| Email list size + weekly growth | growth | Email tool |

**The one report Jason needs most:** the weekly progress_score delta (already designed in the MCP sync) flagging members who moved **25 → 50+ in the last 7 days.** These are the people breaking the wall on their own — the hottest personal-invite targets, and right now completely invisible.

## 5. The 90-Day Sequence

The Q3 member-identity test is still pending and **gates the per-member beacon** — so Phase 1 deliberately does everything that does *not* require the beacon, and stages the beacon-dependent mechanics for after it lands.

### Phase 1 — Days 0–30: Stop the bleeding, see the board (no beacon required)
1. **Turn on Kajabi native gamification** (points + progress bars + badges). **Leave the leaderboard OFF.**
2. **Build the 7-Day Ignition Sequence** for every *new* Greenhouse join (welcome in 5 min, required intro, magic-moment CTA). This fixes the inflow immediately.
3. **Run the one-time lurker segmentation + "Love Our Lurkers" win-back** on the existing 1,332 (email-first). Establishes a true active baseline.
4. **Add annual Garden billing ($190/yr), default toggle to annual.** Close Founding 50 one-time sales.
5. **Stand up the founder dashboard** in NorthΩr with the 7 metrics (CSV + MCP). Start the weekly 25→50 delta report.
6. **Install the weekly 3-post rhythm + schedule the first monthly live Roundtable.**

### Phase 2 — Days 31–60: Manufacture the first result + ascension rung
7. **Build the free "Rock Rich Starting System" mini-course** (5–7 transcripts, 5–9 min lessons, week-one post gate). This is the magic-moment trigger.
8. **Run the first quarterly paid challenge ($47–97, 14 days)** targeting active Greenhouse — Garden upsell at the finish line. Collect testimonials within 48h.
9. **Launch the "Ranch Hand" recurring premium tier ($47–67/mo);** migrate the 33 Founding 50 in free-for-life; personally invite the top 10 Garden members.
10. **Reverse-trial Garden** to lurkers who logged in within 90 days.
11. **Systematize the TikTok → email → Greenhouse pipeline** (bio-link single-CTA page + comment-to-DM + lead magnet). Start Win Wednesday + the weekly question harvest.

### Phase 3 — Days 61–90: Close the data loop, layer the high-intent mechanics
12. **Ship the completion beacon** (once the Q3 identity test passes) → wire lesson-complete → MCP tag → upgrade trigger.
13. **Turn on beacon-dependent mechanics:** milestone badges from real completion data, the 30%-completion DM, course-completion → Garden/Ranch Hand pitch.
14. **Launch accountability pods** for Garden + Founding 50.
15. **Set the quarterly challenge calendar** (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct) and the narrative lore wrapper. Review the dashboard: is Day-7 activation >20%? Is Garden churn <5%? Adjust before scaling.

## 6. Open Questions / Risks (need Jason's input or a live test)

1. **Q3 member-identity test (the gate).** The whole per-member beacon — and every "personal DM at the high-intent moment" mechanic — depends on it. If it slips, Phase 3 slips. *Decision needed: timeline + fallback (CSV-export manual targeting) for the interim.*
2. **What action actually crosses progress_score 25→50?** The entire first-week CTA hinges on this. **Run a live test**: have Jason perform candidate actions on a test account and watch the score. (Hypothesis: first post or first lesson.)
3. **Challenge price point ($47 vs $97).** Higher price = higher completion (skin-in-the-game) but lower entry %. Test one cohort at each. Also: is a *paid* challenge right for a 725k-warm-but-broke audience, or does a free→paid-upsell challenge convert better for *this* niche? **Live A/B.**
4. **Founding 50 transition consent.** Migrating 33 one-time buyers into a recurring tier "free for life" is a trust move — confirm the messaging so it reads as a *gift*, not a bait-and-switch.
5. **Founder time budget.** The weekly 3-post rhythm + monthly live + first-post replies is real recurring labor. Decide what Jason does personally vs. what an anchor member (Joleen) or automation handles — over-committing and going dark is worse than a lighter sustainable cadence.
6. **Email deliverability on the 1,332 purge.** The sunset/"should I stop?" email before pruning is necessary for list health, but cap re-engagement sends at ~10% of daily volume and run it *separate* from the main broadcast to protect sender reputation.
7. **Kajabi "first post" trigger gap.** Confirmed: no native trigger. First-post recognition needs Zapier or the beacon. Decide the bridge before promising "personal reply within 24h" at scale.
8. **The silent-success MCP bug** (create_post / create_announcement return error on success). Any automation that posts to Kajabi via MCP must **check admin before retrying** — never double-post.
